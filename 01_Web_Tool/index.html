SME Design Constraints

The Dual Constraints This Tool Addresses

The SLR found that most AI frameworks fail SMEs because they ignore two co-occurring structural barriers. Every design decision in this tool — from question language to scoring mode — was made to overcome both.

⏱️

Constraint 1

Resource Constraint

"Most frameworks assume organisational capabilities that are unrealistic for SMEs" — Finch & Holmes (2025)

SMEs lack the time, budget and dedicated AI teams that large enterprise frameworks assume. Comprehensive assessments designed for Fortune 500 organisations are simply unworkable for a 20-person firm.

How this tool responds:

  • → Practitioner Mode (24 questions, ~5 min) — deliberately lightweight for resource-constrained SMEs with no specialist knowledge required
  • → Lean scoring — Yes/Partial/No replaces complex Likert scales, removing the need for data analysis expertise
  • → Tiered roadmap — 30/60/90-day prioritised actions scaled to SME capacity, not enterprise transformation programmes
🧠

Constraint 2

Capability & Knowledge Constraint

"Limited awareness of ethical principles, insufficient resources for risk assessment, and lack of specialised AI governance expertise" — SLR (2025)

SME owners and managers often lack formal AI training. Frameworks written for data scientists or compliance officers create a knowledge barrier that prevents meaningful self-assessment.

How this tool responds:

  • → Inline guidance on every academic question — plain-English explanations of what each question means and why it matters
  • → Contextual hints on every practitioner question — normalise partial capability ("a simple checklist counts")
  • → Pillar Coverage chart in results — shows how scores map to the 5 research pillars, bridging practice and theory
